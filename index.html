👤 Q5: Can the AI send emails or legal notices autonomously without oversight?

📁 Q6: How does ZUKWEND handle clients with multiple overdue invoices simultaneously?

A: ZUKWEND automatically consolidates open items for the same debtor via strict 100% Tax ID / VAT verification, creating a single, permanent client dossier under output/CLIENTS/{CLIENT_VAT}/{DATE}/. It generates a single Consolidated Statement of Account (STATEMENT_OF_ACCOUNT_CONSOLIDATED.pdf) with itemized breakdown and currency-partitioned subtotals (e.g. EUR, USD), alongside a single unified email notice (REMINDER_EMAIL_CONSOLIDATED.txt), eliminating duplicate and fragmented reminders. Individual invoices are neatly archived in the Individual_Invoices_Archive/ subfolder.
