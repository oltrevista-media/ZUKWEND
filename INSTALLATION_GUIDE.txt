1. QUICK START (TIER 1 NATIVE ENGINE)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ZUKWEND is designed to work immediately out-of-the-box with zero setup:

1. Extract the "Zukwend_Full" ZIP archive to any local directory (e.g., C:\Zukwend\).
2. Double-click "ZUKWEND.exe" to launch the application.
3. Click "Select Invoice File / Folder" in the main interface to choose your target PDF invoices (ready-to-test sample invoices are provided in the "demo\" folder).
4. Click "Run Document Analysis". In ~1 second, ZUKWEND extracts invoice data, calculates aging risk, generates branded PDF reports, and prepares 1-click email reminders.

No internet connection, external servers, or API keys are required for Tier 1 operation.

3. AI EXECUTION ENGINES: THE 3-TIER ARCHITECTURE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ZUKWEND provides three flexible execution tiers to match your corporate privacy and performance requirements:

[TIER 1] ZUKWEND CORE AI (Default — 100% Offline & Instant)
- How it works: Built-in deterministic rules and local AI parser.
- Setup required: NONE.
- Privacy: 100% Air-Gapped and Local. Zero data transmitted anywhere.
- Speed: ~1 second per invoice.

[TIER 2] LOCAL OLLAMA POWER-UP (Optional — Local Open-Weights LLMs)
- How it works: Connects to your local Ollama server for advanced open-weights reasoning (e.g., Qwen2.5, Gemma3, DeepSeek-R1).
- Setup steps:
  1. Download and install Ollama from https://ollama.com
  2. Open your Command Prompt / Terminal and pull your preferred model:
     Example: "ollama pull qwen2.5:7b-instruct" or "ollama pull gemma3"
  3. Launch ZUKWEND, open "Settings" in the top bar, enable "Local Ollama Engine", select your model from localhost:11434, and click "Save".
- Privacy: 100% Local. Processing stays entirely on your hardware.

[TIER 3] CLOUD BYOC (Optional — Bring Your Own API Key)
- How it works: Allows enterprise teams to utilize top-tier cloud LLMs (OpenAI GPT-4o, Anthropic Claude 3.5, Google Gemini 1.5/2.0, Mistral).
- Setup steps:
  1. Open "Settings" in the ZUKWEND top menu bar.
  2. Select "Cloud BYOC Provider" and choose your provider.
  3. Paste your personal API Key into the designated field and click "Save".
- Privacy: API calls are sent securely directly from your machine to your chosen provider via encrypted TLS. No middleman servers used.

4. CONSOLIDATED CLIENT DOSSIERS & FOLDER HIERARCHY (v2.1.6)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ZUKWEND v2.1.6 introduces enterprise Client Dossier Automation:

Strict P.IVA / Tax ID Matching & Anti-Data Leak:
- Multiple invoices for the same debtor are consolidated into a unified client dossier ONLY when the VAT Registration Number / Tax ID matches 100%.
- If a VAT ID is missing, incomplete, or doubtful (< 5 characters), ZUKWEND automatically falls back to an isolated single dossier, preventing any cross-client data leak.
- Windows-illegal folder characters (/ \ : * ? " < > | &) are automatically sanitized and folder names are limited to 40 characters maximum.

Permanent Folder Structure (dossier_mode = True):
output/
├── CLIENTS/
│   └── {CLIENT_SLUG_VAT}/
│       └── {YYYY-MM-DD}/
│           ├── STATEMENT_OF_ACCOUNT_CONSOLIDATED.pdf  (Consolidated statement table)
│           ├── REMINDER_EMAIL_CONSOLIDATED.txt        (Unified email: SUBJECT + BODY)
│           └── Individual_Invoices_Archive/           (Clean archival subfolder)
│               ├── report_AR_INV-001_{TS}.pdf
│               ├── report_AR_INV-001_{TS}.xlsx
│               └── report_AR_INV-001_{TS}.json
├── MASTER_SUMMARY_AGING_{YYYYMMDD}.xlsx               (Global batch Excel)
└── master_erp_ingestion_{YYYYMMDD}.json               (Global batch JSON payload)

Multi-Currency Partitioning:
- Outstanding invoices are listed line-by-line.
- Subtotals are strictly partitioned per currency (e.g. Total EUR: € 18,450.00 | Total USD: $ 4,200.00).
- Currencies are NEVER algebraically summed together, preserving accounting accuracy.

5. COMPANY BRANDING & SETTINGS CONFIGURATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Customize ZUKWEND to match your corporate identity for generated reports and collection notices:
1. Click the "⚙️ Settings & Branding" button in the top navigation bar.
2. Enter your company details: Legal Business Name, Address, IBAN, and Admin Email.
3. Upload your corporate logo (PNG or JPG format) to automatically brand generated PDF reports.
4. Configure Dossier Mode settings:
   - "Attiva Modalità Dossier Cliente Consolidato (Strict P.IVA)": Enabled by default.
   - "Archivia singoli titoli in Individual_Invoices_Archive/": Enabled by default.
5. Configure Watch Directory & Daemon.
6. Click "💾 Salva Configurazione" to persist your settings in zukwend_config.json.

7. AUTOMATED WATCH DIRECTORY DAEMON
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For automated background processing:
1. Open "Settings & Branding" in ZUKWEND.
2. Specify an inbound directory (e.g., C:\Invoices_Inbound\).
3. Toggle "Attiva Monitoraggio Automatico Background (Watch Daemon)".
4. Click Save. ZUKWEND will continuously monitor the folder, automatically processing incoming invoice PDFs in real time and filing them into client dossiers.
